# PR 13.4 sequenced damaging effects

Pinned reference: Pokémon Showdown commit
`b79ce06d06b8f0dc8de8bb348bc9cab5d931553f`, resolved format
`gen9championsvgc2026regmc`, mod `champions`.

## Source findings

- `sim/battle-actions.ts::hitStepMoveHitLoop` owns the repeated-hit loop and
  stops when no target remains able to receive another hit.
- `sim/battle-actions.ts::moveHit` applies self drops and target secondaries
  after damage at lines around 1091–1094. `selfDrops` and `secondaries` are
  separate paths, so Rotom Spark keeps their declarative payloads distinct.
- `sim/battle-actions.ts::applyRecoilDamage` calculates recoil from the move's
  accumulated actual damage. It is invoked after the hit loop around line 979.
- `sim/battle.ts::spreadDamage` uses the target damage actually applied for
  drain. For generations after 4, lines 2174–2176 use rounded fractional
  healing rather than truncation.
- Representative definitions come from `data/moves.ts`: Body Press (1572),
  Bullet Seed (2004), Double-Edge (3879), Facade (5032), Giga Drain (6559),
Population Bomb, Psyshock (14264), and Seismic Toss (15991). Their structured fields are
  exported instead of transcribing move IDs into the executor.

## Rotom Spark mapping

The generated schema stores structured hit sequences, fixed damage, drain and
recoil fractions, target secondaries, self effects, conditional-power tags,
and offensive/defensive stat overrides. The executor treats these as mechanics
instructions and routes all resulting state writes through `StateKernel`.

The deterministic fixture `data/fixtures/differential/sequenced-effects.json`
is regenerated by `tools/showdown-reference/sequenced-effects.cjs`. It calls
the pinned `Battle.actions.useMove` implementation for Facade, Flamethrower,
Bullet Seed, Population Bomb, Seismic Toss, Close Combat, Chloroblast, Giga Drain, and Double-Edge with
controlled semantic random outcomes. It does not claim raw PRNG parity or a
complete battle-transition comparison.

## Deferred shapes

Charge/recharge state, self-KO timing, OHKO behavior, delayed attacks,
random/scripted targets, move calls, switch control, and custom callbacks remain unsupported.
The exporter marks unrepresentable secondary or self-effect shapes as special
behavior so capability preflight rejects them explicitly.
