# PR 15.5 — generic item and ability lifecycles

## Pinned behavioral basis

All behavior in this PR was checked against Pokémon Showdown commit
`b79ce06d06b8f0dc8de8bb348bc9cab5d931553f`, Champions format
`gen9championsvgc2026regmc`.

Primary source locations:

- `sim/pokemon.ts:Pokemon.takeItem` — removal eligibility, item End and
  AfterTakeItem ordering.
- `sim/pokemon.ts:Pokemon.setItem` — atomic replacement and item End/Start.
- `sim/pokemon.ts:Pokemon.setAbility` — old ability End, replacement, new
  ability Start.
- `sim/pokemon.ts:Pokemon.ignoringAbility` and `Pokemon.ignoringItem` —
  persistent suppression and move-scoped ignoring.
- `data/moves.ts:Moves.knockoff`, `Moves.trick`, and `Moves.switcheroo` — item
  removal, power, exchange, Sticky Hold, and failure semantics.
- `data/abilities.ts:Abilities.magician`, `pickpocket`, `stickyhold`,
  `unburden`, `trace`, `mummy`, and `wanderingspirit` — representative
  lifecycle consumers.

The normalized complete-turn fixture is
`data/fixtures/differential/item-ability-lifecycle.json` and is regenerated by
`tools/showdown-reference/item-ability-lifecycle.cjs`.

## Rotom Spark ownership

The kernel owns atomic item removal, transfer, exchange, consumption history,
ability replacement/swap, suppression state, and switch-out restoration.
Mechanics decide whether Sticky Hold, a Mega Stone, Unburden, or an ability
replacement rule permits the requested operation.

Ability handlers no longer replace Mummy, Wandering Spirit, or Trace inline.
They append typed transition requests to the completed event dispatch. Combat
then applies `AbilityEnd -> kernel mutation -> entry effects -> AbilityStart`,
which prevents a handler collection from changing underneath the event that
requested the transition.

`last_item` follows Showdown's consumed-item history: theft, Knock Off, and
exchange do not populate it. `item_used_this_turn` is reset after residual
processing. Unburden has separate authoritative activation state and therefore
does not activate merely because a Pokémon entered battle without an item.

## Scope boundaries

- Knock Off, Trick, and Switcheroo are executable through the canonical move
  lifecycle in this PR.
- Generic persistent suppression storage and event filtering are present.
  Gastro Acid and Neutralizing Gas trigger behavior remain identity-specific
  work because their moves/field behavior are not yet executable.
- Pickup's target selection, Ability Shield restrictions, Trace's complete
  `notrace` filter, and unusual immutable-ability flags remain attached to
  their individual mechanics. They are not silently treated as verified
  identities by this PR.
- Choice-lock cleanup and broader move history remain PR 15.6 capabilities.
