# Base combat research

Reference: Pokémon Showdown commit
`b79ce06d06b8f0dc8de8bb348bc9cab5d931553f`, Champions mod,
`gen9championsvgc2026regmc`.

## Source paths

- `data/mods/champions/scripts.ts` — `statModify` defines Champions stat
  points, nature rounding, and the Level Clause Mod formula; `calculatePP`
  defines Champions PP scaling.
- `sim/battle-actions.ts` — `hitStepAccuracy`, `getDamage`, and
  `modifyDamage` define accuracy stages, offensive/defensive stat selection,
  base damage, critical damage, random damage, STAB, and type ordering.
- `sim/battle.ts` — `modify` uses a 4096-based modifier with half-down
  rounding; `randomizer` selects an integer percentage from 85 through 100.
- `data/typechart.ts` — resolved attack/defense type relations. The exporter
  now records the resolved Champions chart instead of duplicating it by hand.

## Implemented slice

Rotom supports ordinary, selected-target attacks for a small audited set:
`Aqua Jet`, `Aqua Tail`, `Dragon Pulse`, `High Horsepower`, `Power Gem`,
`Power Whip`, and `Seed Bomb`. These moves have no secondary or custom move
behavior in the pinned source. Other moves fail explicitly until their effects
are implemented.

Accuracy, critical hits, and the 85–100 damage roll consume `BattleRng`.
Move PP is deducted before accuracy. Damage applies critical, random, STAB,
then type modifiers in Showdown order. Immunities deal zero; other calculated
zeroes become one damage.

The focused fixture at `data/fixtures/differential/basic-damage.json` is
generated by `tools/showdown-reference/basic-damage.cjs` with critical hits
disabled and the damage roll forced to 100 percent.

## Deferred behavior

Spread/random targeting, retargeting after a target leaves or faints, Struggle,
move secondary effects, callbacks, ability/item/weather damage modifiers,
burn, and post-turn residual processing remain explicit unsupported or later
event-framework work.
